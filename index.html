        Lower usage without guesswork

        Start with the appliances that run longest or draw the most power. Small habit changes matter most when repeated every day.

        01

        Move heavy use off peak

        If your meter has time-of-use pricing, run ironing, pumping and washing in off-peak hours.

        02

        Set cooling carefully

        Use an efficient temperature, clean filters and close gaps around doors before lowering the thermostat further.

        03

        Measure standby load

        Turn off chargers, set-top boxes and spare appliances at the socket when they will be unused for long periods.

        04

        Track units weekly

        Photograph the meter on the same day each week. A sudden jump helps identify a failing appliance or changed habit.

        05

        Maintain high-load devices

        Service pumps, refrigerators and air conditioners. Blocked airflow and worn motors can increase consumption.

        06

        Check the official bill

        Compare current and previous readings, billing days, tariff category, arrears and variable adjustments before paying.

        Good questions before you pay.

        Official bills can include adjustments that a general estimator cannot know. These answers explain what to verify.

        Is this the final amount I must pay?

        No. This is an estimate based on the selected tariff profile and your inputs. Meter multipliers, late fees, arrears, subsidies, duties and monthly adjustments can change the official bill.

        What does “protected” mean in Pakistan?

        For electricity, protected residential status generally requires usage at or below 200 units per month over the previous six months. Gas protected status depends on winter consumption history. Confirm the category printed on your bill.

        Why is fuel adjustment not fixed?

        Fuel cost adjustment changes by billing month. Enter the rate shown in the applicable notification or on your bill; leaving it at zero excludes it from the estimate.

        Does the India calculator include free-unit schemes?

        No. It shows simplified pre-subsidy energy charges. Eligibility, caps and application rules vary by state, discom and household.

        Is my usage saved?

        No. Inputs and results exist only in the current tab. Downloading a summary saves a file only when you choose to do so.
